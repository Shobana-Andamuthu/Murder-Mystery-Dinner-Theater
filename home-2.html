Culinary Evidence

The Gastronomic Heist Tasting Menu

Four secretive courses crafted by Michelin-trained culinarians, each paired with an authentic prohibition cocktail containing clues to the murderer's identity.

First Course • Served At Arrival

Truffled Forest Mushroom Tartlet

Flaky butter pastry with aged Gruyère, thyme-seared golden chanterelles, and delicate Umbrian black truffle glaze. Presented inside a mini evidence lockbox.

• 100% Vegetarian Alternative Available
Prohibition Cocktail Pairing

The Bootlegger's French 75

Artisanal London dry gin, fresh Meyer lemon juice, cane syrup, and topped with vintage French Champagne. Served in a chilled vintage coupe with edible gold dust.

Second Course • The Lights Flicker

Smoked Duck Breast Carpaccio

Hickory-smoked Magret duck thinly shaved with blood-orange reduction, pickled heirloom shallots, watercress, and toasted black walnut crumble.

• Glazed Roasted Heirloom Beet alternative for vegan guests
Prohibition Cocktail Pairing

The Crimson Alibi

Campari, Sweet Vermouth, rye whiskey, blood-orange bitters, infused with smoke under a glass cloche lifted tableside.

Third Course • The Interrogation Entrée

Slow-Braised Prime Angus Short Rib

Braised for 14 hours in Cabernet Sauvignon, served over silky pomme purée, glazed rainbow carrots, and bone-marrow jus.

• Wild Chilean Sea Bass or Truffle Mushroom Wellington available
Prohibition Cocktail Pairing

The Godfather's Manhattan

Small-batch Kentucky bourbon, Carpano Antica, Italian Amaro, and brandied Luxardo cherries aged in oak casks.

Fourth Course • Accusation & Reveal

The Valrhona "Poison" Sphere

A fragile sphere of 70% dark chocolate melted tableside with hot spiced salted caramel to expose Madagascar vanilla bean gelato and wild raspberry coulis.

• Coconut Dairy-Free Gelato alternative provided
Prohibition Cocktail Pairing

The Midnight Espresso Cordial

Fresh pulled espresso, Kahlúa, spiced dark rum, and vanilla liqueur topped with shaved chocolate and roasted coffee beans.
