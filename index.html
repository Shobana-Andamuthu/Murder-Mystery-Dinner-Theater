How The Mystery Unfolds Across 4 Acts

You are neither a passive spectator nor an outsider. At The Grand Enigma, you dine at the crime scene, interrogate the suspects between courses, and vote on the final verdict.

I

Arrival & Suspect Mingling

Step past the velvet rope into the 1920s foyer. Characters in period costume converse with your table, dropping subtle motives, whispered rivalries, and veiled alibis.

Course 1: Truffled Mushroom
II

The First Course & The Murder

As appetizers are served, the lights flicker and an argument breaks out. A sudden blackout, a blood-curdling scream, and a prominent guest collapses. The venue is sealed.

Course 2: Smoked Duck Breast
III

The Entrée & Interrogation

Sealed evidence folders arrive at your table: autopsy reports, forged letters, and telegrams. Suspects visit your table for direct questioning while you enjoy your main entrée.

Course 3: Prime Angus Short Rib
IV

Accusation & The Verdict Reveal

Each table casts an official ballot identifying the murderer, weapon, and motive. The detective reveals the shocking truth, and top sleuth tables win the Golden Masque trophy.
