Guest Experience Architecture

How You Solve The Murder Over Dinner

You are never pulled on stage or forced to speak in public. Investigation rounds unfold seamlessly between courses at your own pace.

1

Course 1: The Evidence Dossier Unsealed

As appetizers are served, each table receives a wax-sealed confidential case file containing suspect biographies, floor plans, and the initial police bulletin.

2

Course 2: The Murder Incident Live

During soup and salad, tensions erupt in the dining room. A gunshot or poisoned cry rings out. The victim collapses, and the Chief Inspector locks down the hall.

3

Course 3: Tabletop Interrogation Rounds

Between the main entrée and palate cleansers, surviving suspects visit every table for 3-minute cross-examinations. Grill them on their motives and alibis.

4

Course 4: Final Verdict & Award Presentation

Before dessert, tables cast their official Detective Ballots. The Inspector names the killer, reconstructs the crime, and awards the Golden Handcuffs trophy to winning sleuths.
