THE ANATOMY OF A MURDER SOIRÉE

A seamless three-hour theatrical odyssey where gastronomy and suspense advance in perfect lockstep. Every course brings new alibis, interrogations, and dramatic revelations.

06:30 PM 1

Speakeasy Entrance & Alibi Cocktails

Whisper the evening's secret passphrase at the heavy brass bookcase door. Sip signature prohibition cocktails while actors in character mingle disguised among guests.

Welcome Cocktail: The Smoked Rye Alibi
07:15 PM 2

Act I: The Gathering & Amuse-Bouche

Guests are seated at velvet-clothed banquet tables. The family patriarch or host delivers a scandalous welcome address before tensions suddenly flare into high drama.

Truffled Wild Mushroom Tartlet & Bisque
07:45 PM 3

Act II: The Blackout & The Murder

Lights flicker and a blood-curdling scream shatters the silence. The victim collapses center stage. The Lead Detective locks all exits as the decadent chef entrée is served to tables.

Prime Filet Mignon or Pan-Seared Seabass
08:45 PM 4

Act III: Clue Hunt & Interrogations

Your table receives sealed forensic dossiers, cipher wheels, and coroner reports. Actors are brought directly to your table to be cross-examined under intense scrutiny.

Detective Clue Dossier & Interrogation Pack
09:30 PM 5

Act IV: The Climax, Dessert & Accusations

Submit your formal accusation ballot with dessert. The culprit is sensationally unmasked, handcuffed, and top sleuths receive custom engraved awards.

Gold-Dusted Belgian Dark Chocolate Ganache
