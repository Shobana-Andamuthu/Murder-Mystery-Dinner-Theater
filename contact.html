Frequently Answered Questions

We encourage cocktail or period-appropriate 1920s–1940s vintage formal wear (suits, blazers, evening gowns, cloche hats, and pocket squares). While strict black-tie is not mandatory, athletic wear, beachwear, and casual t-shirts are respectfully prohibited in the auditorium.
