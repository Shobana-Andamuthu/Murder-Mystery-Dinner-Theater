The Theatrical Process

BESPOKE SCRIPT CUSTOMIZATION

Our resident dramatists collaborate with your event committee to integrate company lore, playful executive rivalries, or inside jokes directly into the murder mystery script.

01

Executive Briefing

A 30-minute discovery call where our scriptwriter gathers context: department inside jokes, company milestones, and the personalities of key colleagues.

02

Suspect Casting

Select 2 to 5 VIP colleagues or executives to be cast as suspects or surprise secret agents. They receive confidential briefing dossiers prior to arrival.

03

Branded Evidence

We design custom forensic evidence dossiers, cipher documents, and coroner certificates watermarked with your corporate insignia or family crest.

04

Grand Accusation

During dessert, ballot cards are counted. The villain is sensationally handcuffed, and top detective tables are presented with engraved magnifying trophies.
