THE FOUR-ACT GASTRONOMIC PROGRESSION

Each course is served in synchronization with the theater performance. Choose your personal selections at your table upon arrival with your dedicated Table Captain.

Act I • 07:15 PM

The Prelude & Amuse-Bouche

A delicate introductory tasting featuring velvety wild chanterelle velouté and cherrywood-smoked duck carpaccio.

Act II • 07:45 PM

The Cold-Blooded Starters

Savor cast-iron seared Atlantic diver scallops paired with heirloom roasted golden beets and burrata stracciatella.

Act III • 08:30 PM

The Climax & Main Entrées

Prime 45-day dry-aged filet mignon alongside wild-caught Chilean sea bass accompanied by saffron risotto.

Act IV • 09:30 PM

The Final Verdict Desserts

Conclude with an artisan dark Valrhona chocolate sphere and refreshing organic Meyer lemon lavender tart.

OUR PURVEYORS & REGIONAL SOURCING

We partner with generational family farms, regenerative cattle ranches, and day-boat docks committed to cruelty-free stewardship and organic agricultural practices.

Beef & Poultry

Seafood

Atlantic Day-Boat Docks
